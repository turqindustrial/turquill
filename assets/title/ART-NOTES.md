# Title art

## The emblem

`turquill-seal-source.webp` (1254 × 1212) is the host's own art, supplied on 2026-10-10 with the words "make this the logo and title screen and animate it": on a dark plum-black ground, a crimson scaled serpent with a dragon's head (at the top, facing right) bites its tail round a large faceted crimson Q whose tail is a dagger; the blade crosses the ring towards the lower right. Four-point stars sit on the ring at west, east and south (the north star crowns the Q), with a sun and moons on a thin inner circle. It replaced the drawn serpent (`serpent.js`, retired) and the lettered Q seal everywhere: the launch card, the title page, the story-loading seal, the top bar, the boot card, the pairing page and the app icons. It is the host's, not generated by the game.

### The Turquill emblem: a sword and a quill (2026-10-26)

With the rename to Turquill the host asked for a new logo ("create a new logo for it"), first a T whose upright is a quill, then "maybe a quill and a sword", "abandon the T shape of it" and, of three drawings (a saltire, a sword point-down with a quill across it, a quill nib-down with a sword behind), the saltire with a "Finer sword". The serpent ring is the painting's, unchanged; only the still layer is new.

`node make-turquill-core.cjs` (headless Chrome or Edge, no internet) takes the Q with its glow, its top spike and the dagger with its glow out of `core-v2.webp` (feathered at the edge; the suns, moons and the small diamond are put back) and draws a sword and a quill crossed in their place, 36 degrees from upright: the sword's hilt to the upper left (a small star pommel, a long wrapped grip, a thin straight crossguard tapering to diamond finials, a gem on a lozenge), its slender fullered blade's point, with a gleam, to the lower right; the quill's pointed feather to the upper right (both vanes, deep splits along the barbs, a bright rachis), its bare shaft and split nib with a drop of ink to the lower left; a faceted four-point star with a diamond where they cross. Each part is drawn upright and laid on its diagonal, its lighting turned back so the light still falls from the upper left; the parts carry chiselled bevels (a bright edge toward the light, a dark one away) and the painted Q's marbled stone (mottling, veins, pale streaks, fine cracks), and a colour pass matches their median saturation, mean green and value to the Q as painted (the painting cut as make-emblem.cjs cuts it), hues turned 8 degrees toward its crimson-rose. Its report gives the balance (centroid and quadrants about the middle) and checks that nothing lies on the suns, the moons, the small diamond or the inner circle's arcs. It writes `public/emblem/core-v3.webp`; `node make-turquill-emblem.cjs` then makes `core-glow-v3.webp` (256 px, blurred), `seal-v2.webp` (264 px whole: the top bar), `seal-core-v2.webp` (360 px: the boot card and the pairing page, over `seal-ring-v1.webp`) and `turquill-emblem.webp` here (900 px whole: the app icons and native builds). The fourth twinkle is now on the star at the crossing (TitleEmblem.js `centreStar`), the sheen sweeps across the still layer, and the icons were redrawn (`?v=5`). The drawings that were not chosen, and the T, are not kept in the repository.

`node make-emblem.cjs` (headless Chrome or Edge, no internet) prepares it. The square is cut round the ring's centre (622, 588 in the source; the serpent's outer edge is 520 from it, its inner edge 451, the head and the south star reach 612). The dark ground becomes transparency by colour to alpha against the painting's own background (20, 1, 15), so on the game's dark backdrop the emblem looks as painted. The picture is then split:

- `public/emblem/ring-v2.webp`: everything beyond the inner circle, and the parts of the ring stars and of the dragon's jaw that reach in across it. Under the dagger's blade this layer holds scales from further round the ring (a scale row comes about every 7.1°, but the painting is drawn by hand): `make-emblem.cjs` measures which turn (9° to 32° either way) and which small step in or out makes the scales match the serpent just beside the blade best (now 13.95° round, 2 px in), and blends that copy in over 8 pixels past the blade's outline. The blade's soft glow beside the serpent's body (14 px inside and outside the ring) belongs to the still layer. So when the ring turns nothing of the blade goes with it, and no seam or faint streak travels round (checked on 2026-10-10, when the turn became faster).
- `public/emblem/core-v2.webp`: the Q, the inner circle with its sun, moons and dots, and the whole blade (its edges measured from the painting: `y = x − 39` and `y = 499.5 + x/2` meeting at the point, 1077, 1038), taken with its dark outline; over the serpent the blade is kept exactly as painted (a shadow darker than the ground cannot be made of transparency).
- Two small blurred copies for the breathing glow, `seal-v1.webp` (264 px, the whole emblem: the top bar), `seal-ring-v1.webp` and `seal-core-v1.webp` (360 px ring and core layers: the boot card and the pairing page, whose ring turns too) and `painted-emblem.webp` here (the painting's whole emblem; it was the app icons' until the sword and quill). Since the sword and quill, only the ring pictures (`ring-v2`, `ring-glow-v2`, `seal-ring-v1`) are shown; the still layer's are `core-v3`, `core-glow-v3`, `seal-v2`, `seal-core-v2` and `turquill-emblem.webp` (see above).

The game turns the ring all the way round once every 36 s (it was 90 s until the host asked on 2026-10-10 to "make sure the ouroboros animation goes all the way around the circle"), steadily, clockwise, the way the dragon faces, every emblem on the page on one clock (TitleEmblem.js `emblemPhase`, a negative animation delay), so a change of screen never turns it back, under the still sword and quill; the glow breathes, a light sweeps over them and the stars twinkle (webTheme.js `[data-qb=emblem]`). The Light atmosphere, reduced motion and full-screen covers hold it still. The colour themes tint it with a CSS filter (themeRules.js `emblem`). Gold is not a hue turn (that left the crimson olive and amber) but metal: `grayscale(1) sepia(1) saturate(2.4) hue-rotate(-2deg) brightness(1.4) contrast(1.25)`, the painting's light and shade toned to pale yellow-gold highlights and deep bronze shadows. When the painting changes, run `node make-emblem.cjs`, then `node make-turquill-core.cjs`, `node make-turquill-emblem.cjs` and `node make-icons.cjs`, and raise `-vN` in the file names and `?v=N` in the icon links; when only the sword and quill change, start at `node make-turquill-core.cjs`.

## The title painting

`turquill-title.jpg` (1536 × 1024) is the backdrop of the title screen and the menus. It was painted on 2026-10-02 by the game's own illustration service (`world-art.cjs`, kind `landscape`) from this description:

> A vast fantasy valley at dusk seen from a high ridge. On the right-hand third of the picture a lone cloaked traveller stands on a rocky outcrop holding a small warm lantern, seen from behind, small against the land. Beyond them a pale road winds down through dark pine woods past a stone bridge over a silver river toward a ruined round tower on a crag, and far snow mountains under towering storm-lit clouds with a break of gold light. The left-hand third of the picture is quiet, dark forested hillside in deep teal shadow with very little detail. Everything runs edge to edge: a full wide panorama with no frame, no vignette border and no empty margins.

The two battle paintings that replaced it later on 2026-10-02 (four heroes against a lich, one wide and one tall) were retired on 2026-10-04 at the host's request, and this painting came back. They remain in the repository's history.

### How it is shown (`App.js`, `HomeScreen.js` `homeLayout`, `webTheme.js`)

- The picture always covers the window (`resizeMode="cover"`; `s.backdrop` sets width and height to 100%). An image loaded from a file carries its own pixel size as its style, and without that the picture once stopped short and left wide windows black on the right.
- Wide screens and a phone on its side (`data-frame=wide`, from `homeLayout(width,height).wide`): the view sits at 50% 42%, the horizon. The left third is dark on purpose: the name and the menu sit there (`atmosphere-home` darkens it a little more). The saved hero is a slim strip in the bottom right corner, below the lantern-bearer's feet (on a 1366 × 768 laptop the strip is at about y 650 to 712, the traveller's feet at about y 562).
- An upright phone (`data-frame=tall`): only a strip of the picture fits, so the view moves to 62% 50%, the tower, the bridge and the peak. The name sits above, the hero strip and the compact menu below (`atmosphere-home-narrow` darkens the bottom for them).
- The painting drifts in only a little (`qb-drift`, scale 1 to 1.05: the host asked for it zoomed out on 2026-10-02) and holds still in the Light atmosphere.

## The app icon

The app icons are the page `icon.html` in this folder: the emblem (`turquill-emblem.webp`) on the game's dark launch glow, drawn offline. `node make-icons.cjs` photographs it at each size with a headless Chrome or Edge (home screen, manifest, browser tab and the Android adaptive layers). The 192, 180 and 48 px icons are cut close to the serpent ring (`simple`) so the sword and quill stay readable; the monochrome layer is the emblem's silhouette. After changing the icon, raise `?v=N` in the icon links (`public/manifest.json`, `public/index.html`, `fullscreen.js`, the pairing page in `phone-server.cjs`; `?v=5` since the sword and quill, 2026-10-26; `?v=4` from 2026-10-10) so installed copies and browser tabs fetch the new one. An iPhone keeps the icon it was added with: remove the home-screen icon and add it again.
